FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does White Coat Studio offer for medical practices?

We provide a comprehensive suite of healthcare marketing services including local SEO for clinics, high-end video production, patient booking portals, and targeted lead generation for patient acquisition.

How long does it take to see patient growth?

The timeline varies based on your practice's goals. A typical medical marketing strategy phase takes 2-4 weeks, followed by implementation and continuous optimization to attract and retain patients.

How do you measure the success of a healthcare campaign?

We track key performance indicators such as patient acquisition cost (PAC), appointment booking rates, and return on ad spend to ensure we meet your specific practice goals.

Will my marketing strategy be customized for my medical specialty?

Absolutely. We don't believe in one-size-fits-all solutions. Every strategy is tailored to your specific medical specialty, local patient demographics, and practice objectives.

What is your pricing model for healthcare marketing?

We offer flexible pricing models including project-based fees for website redesigns and monthly retainers for ongoing SEO and ad management. We'll work with you to align with your practice's growth goals.
